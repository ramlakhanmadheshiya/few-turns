## Short description (80 max)

## Full description (4000 max)

Few Turns is a calm arrow puzzle. Each room has a start, a few red dots and a home. You get a handful of arrows: tap a cell to lay one, tap again to turn it, or swipe the way it should point. Press Go and the orb rolls, turning on every arrow. Take every dot, then roll home.

Miss, and the orb goes back to the start. Your arrows stay where you laid them, and the missed path stays faintly on the board with the reason, so you can see what went wrong and adjust. Get home on the first try for three stars.

NOT ONE TURN SPARE
Every room gives you the fewest arrows that can solve it. A solver has checked all 360 rooms: each can be done with exactly the turns it gives, and not with fewer.

NINE IDEAS, ONE AT A TIME
Each chapter adds one new idea and opens with a short tutorial room:
• Path: arrows, dots, blocks and home
• Signs: printed arrows that turn the orb too
• Bank: bends that bank the orb round the corner
• Gate: a switch that flips every gate
• Spin: spinners that turn a quarter after every pass
• Shift: portals that carry the orb across the room
• Crumble: cracked floor that holds only once
• Count: numbered dots that only count in order
• Pair: two orbs that share your arrows

360 ROOMS, A STEADY CLIMB
Every chapter has 40 rooms. They start gentle and grow harder, with the number of turns climbing from one to five.

CALM BY DESIGN
• Just one short ad every 10 levels, and none mid-level
• Optional videos only when you choose them
• Three free hints a day; a hint outlines where every arrow goes
• Progress stays on your device, with no account to make

SHARE YOUR RUNS
After a clear, save or share a picture of the room and the run you found, and ask your friends: can you find the turns?
